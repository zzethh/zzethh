### 🛠️ Featured Projects

#### 🔬 [**LumiCompose — End-to-End Product Scene Generation**](https://github.com/zzethh/LumiCompose-End-to-End-Product-Scene-Generation-with-Semantic-Lighting-Harmonization)
> *Python, PyTorch, Stable Diffusion, LoRA, U-Net, Cross-Attention, FastAPI, Streamlit*

* Engineered an integrated AI studio merging **Stable Diffusion Inpainting** with **LoRA fine-tuning** and a novel **Semantic-Gated U-Net** for single-click product background replacement and lighting harmonization, serving **3 generation modes** and **3 harmonization models** on a unified GPU pipeline.
* Implemented **High-Res Residual Blending** and **Dual-Energy Residual Gating**, achieving an **8.6% foreground MSE reduction** on **38,545** HCOCO images with pixel-perfect foreground preservation at original resolution.
* Deployed a production-grade **FastAPI + Streamlit** service with real-time **Prometheus + Grafana** telemetry tracking **5 custom metrics** including inference latency and CLIP scores.

#### 🎨 [**Gen-AI Studio — Product Background Replacement**](https://github.com/zzethh/Gen-AI-Studio-Product-Background-Replacement)
> *Python, PyTorch, Stable Diffusion, LoRA, FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker, MLflow, Grafana*

* Developed an AI product background replacement system using **Stable Diffusion Inpainting v1.5** with **PEFT LoRA** fine-tuning on **44,000+** product images from HuggingFace.
* Trained **2 LoRA adapters** (350 and 44,000 steps) to quantify **Catastrophic Forgetting**, achieving a **CLIP Score of approximately 28.4** on the baseline model.
* Deployed on **Google Cloud (NVIDIA T4 GPU)** with a full **MLOps pipeline** — FastAPI, Streamlit, MLflow, Prometheus + Grafana, and Docker Compose.

#### 🖼️ [**Deep Image Harmonization via Semantic-Gated Cross-Attention**](https://github.com/zzethh/Deep-Image-Harmonization-via-Semantic-Gated-Cross-Attention)
> *Python, PyTorch, U-Net, Cross-Attention, Streamlit*

* Proposed a **Semantic-Gated Cross-Attention** mechanism to suppress unrelated attention, reducing **foreground Mean Squared Error (fMSE) by 8.6%** on the **HCOCO** dataset.
* Benchmarked **3 architectures** (U-Net, S2AM, Semantic-Gated) over **100 epochs** on **38,545 images**, achieving a best **fMSE of 466.51** and **Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) of 35.16 dB**.
* Designed a **Lightweight Mask Encoder** with **Residual Gating** to prevent feature collapse, deploying a live demo on **Hugging Face Hub**.
